Using CardVision

Record, analyze, review

Start before cards are dealt, switch to your table during the countdown, and keep recording through the cleared table between rounds. Card identities and provisional counts update as frames are analyzed. Stop halts capture and drains all saved frames before review.

Review each candidate

A row is a candidate physical card, not a confirmed unique card. Its crops are repeated observations of that candidate. Click a crop to inspect the full screenshot. Correct ranks and suits, exclude duplicates or false detections, add missed cards, and adjust order. Cards first visible in the same screenshot have uncertain relative order.

What the count covers

The total starts at zero for each recording and carries across its rounds. Cards already visible at the start are included. Partial captures are not complete rounds. Separate recordings are independent; shoe changes are not detected.

Unknown cards contribute zero to the known-card subtotal and make the full count incomplete. Any displayed range covers detected candidates only; missing or duplicate cards can change it. Check the Rounds and Coverage tabs before saving a review.

Recognition scores

Model and OCR scores are recognition scores, not measured accuracy or a guarantee that a rank is correct. A reviewed result records your corrections, not an independently verified prediction.
